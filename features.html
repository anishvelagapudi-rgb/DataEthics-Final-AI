FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is [APP NAME] free to use?

Yes. [APP NAME] will offer a free, ad-supported tier. A premium subscription (est. $5/month) removes ads and unlocks deeper analysis history and batch scanning.

Does it tell me what's "true" or "false"?

No — and that's intentional. [APP NAME] identifies how information is presented in potentially misleading ways. We flag manipulation patterns, not political truth, to avoid introducing our own bias.

What kinds of manipulation does it detect?

Truncated chart axes, misleading framing, decontextualized statistics, missing comparative data, emotionally loaded language paired with thin evidence, and more. The model is trained on a taxonomy of known misrepresentation techniques.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. No AI is perfect, and [APP NAME] will occasionally produce false positives or miss subtle manipulation. We display a confidence level with each flag and encourage users to apply their own judgment.

Will it work on paywalled content?

If you have access to the content in your browser, [APP NAME] can analyze it. We do not bypass paywalls or access content you don't already have permission to view.
