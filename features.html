Features & Uses

How TruthLens Works

From a suspicious headline to a full breakdown in seconds — here's exactly what TruthLens does and who it helps.

The Process

Analysis in Four Steps

TruthLens doesn't just tell you something is misleading — it walks you through exactly how the manipulation works and what to look for next time.

1

Scan

TruthLens monitors news articles, videos, charts, and social media posts as you browse — automatically and in real time.

2

Analyze

Our AI cross-references content against known patterns of misleading framing, statistical manipulation, distorted graphs, and missing context.

3

Explain

A clear, jargon-free summary tells you exactly what looks suspicious, why it matters, and what a more accurate version of that claim would look like.

4

Learn

Over time, TruthLens helps you internalize media literacy skills so you can spot manipulation yourself — even without the app open.

Core Features

What TruthLens Detects

📰

Article Scanning

Analyzes written content for emotionally charged language, missing attribution, deceptive framing, and statements presented as fact without sourcing.

📊

Data Visualization Check

Identifies manipulated chart baselines, misleading axis scales, cherry-picked date ranges, and graphs that visually exaggerate small differences.

🎬

Audio & Video Analysis

Detects misleading narration, deceptive editing patterns, and clips used out of their original context to imply a different meaning.

⚡

Real-Time Detection

Works live as you browse — no manual copy-pasting or separate tab required. Flags appear inline with the content you're reading.

🔔

Bias Indicators

Rates content on a spectrum from neutral to heavily slanted, providing source context so you understand the outlet's track record and framing patterns.

📚

Manipulation Explanations

Goes beyond "true" or "false" labels to explain the specific tactic being used — so users leave more informed, not just more skeptical.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does TruthLens remove or block content?

No. TruthLens never removes or alters content — it only adds context and analysis alongside what you're already viewing. You always retain full control.

Is TruthLens politically biased?

Our detection methodology focuses on structural manipulation techniques — distorted statistics, missing context, misleading framing — rather than political positions. We publish our criteria openly and accept community feedback on disputed flags.

What platforms does TruthLens work on?

TruthLens is designed as a browser extension (Chrome, Firefox, Edge) and a companion mobile app for iOS and Android, covering news sites, social media, and video platforms.

How accurate is it?

No AI system is perfect. TruthLens flags content for review and always explains its reasoning — the user makes the final call. You can contest any flag through our human review process.

Is it free?

TruthLens offers a free, ad-supported tier. A premium subscription ($5/month) removes ads and unlocks detailed analysis reports. With just 1% of US internet users on the free tier, the model generates around $3M annually — making it financially sustainable at any scale.
