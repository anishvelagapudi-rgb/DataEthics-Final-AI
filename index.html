What We're Building

Fighting Deception at Its Source

Modern misinformation isn't just outright lies; it's manipulated chart baselines, emotionally charged headlines, and statistics stripped of context. TruthLens identifies these tactics and explains them in plain language, building media literacy one article at a time.

Real-Time Scanning

Analyzes news content and media the moment you encounter it, no manual copy-pasting, no waiting.

Chart & Graph Analysis

Detects manipulated scales, cherry-picked date ranges, and misleading visual patterns in data visualizations.

Context Gap Detection

Flags missing context, emotionally charged framing, and statistics presented without proper sourcing or attribution.

Media Literacy Education

Explains why content is misleading, not just that it is, so you build skills to recognize manipulation yourself.
